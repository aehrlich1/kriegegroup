### Temporary statement on accessibility

This page declares the accessibility of the University of Vienna’s websites for people with disabilities. [Further information on digital accessibility](https://www.digitalbarrierefrei.at/de/verstehen/worum-geht-es-bei-digitaler-barrierefreiheit)

### Accessibility status of this website

This website of the University of Vienna was completed in September 2024. Accessibility was a key concern during the technical and editorial revision. Nevertheless, errors cannot be ruled out, especially in the course of a relaunch.

A review is currently being carried out based on the [criteria for digital accessibility](https://www.digitalbarrierefrei.at/en/understanding/accessibility-criteria).

### Scope

Domain: kriegegroup.univie.ac.at (Work Group Page)

Operator: University of Vienna

The accessibility statement for other websites of the University of Vienna that have not yet been converted can be found [here](https://www.univie.ac.at/en/about-us/accessibility/).

### Contact for feedback

If you have any problems with the accessibility of this website, please contact [wzg(at)univie.ac.at](https://www.univie.ac.at/en/about-us/information-on-accessibility/#).

Please describe your problem as precisely as possible and indicate on which page (URL) the problem occurred.

If you do not receive a satisfactory response, you can submit a complaint to the responsible [monitoring body](https://www.digitalbarrierefrei.at/en/submit-a-complaint/complaints-offices-in-austria). [Further information on the complaint procedure](https://www.digitalbarrierefrei.at/en/submit-a-complaint/information-on-the-complaints-process)

### Legal basis

This accessibility statement is based on the obligations set out in the [Web Accessibility Act](https://www.digitalbarrierefrei.at/en/understanding/legal-framework).

Accessibility is checked on the basis of the [criteria of EN 301 549](https://www.digitalbarrierefrei.at/en/understanding/accessibility-criteria).
