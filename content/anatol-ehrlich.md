### Biography

After earning both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Chemical and Process Engineering from ETH Zurich, I conducted my master’s thesis at Stanford University on organic, stretchable semiconductors. Upon returning to Vienna, I spent two years as a project manager at a startup company specializing in oil-in-water measurement devices. Motivated to deepen my expertise in software engineering and machine learning, I pursued an MSc in Computational Science at the University of Vienna while working as a software engineer on the settlement and transaction interface (ASTI) at the Austrian National Bank. I am keen on integrating my expertise in chemistry, software engineering, and artificial intelligence to develop innovative graph-based machine learning models for drug discovery.
