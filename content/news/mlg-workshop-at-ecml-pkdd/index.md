We are happy to announce, that the **22nd Mining and Learning with Graphs** workshop will be held at [ECML/PKDD](https://ecmlpkdd.org/2025/) on ***September 15th 2025*** in Porto!

We will have exciting keynotes by [Rebekka Burgholz](https://sites.google.com/view/rebekkaburkholz/) and [Matthias Fey](https://rusty1s.github.io/#/), as well as plenty of time for socializing.

Submit your work until 14th of June at [http://mlg-europe.github.io](https://t.co/4yFD3aJ5do)!

![](/images/uploads/2025/05/image_porta.jpeg)

The **European Conference on Machine Learning and Principles and Practice of Knowledge Discovery in Databases** will take place from September 15-19, 2025, in Porto, Portugal.
