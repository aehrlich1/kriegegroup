The [kriegegroup.univie.ac.at](https://kriegegroup.univie.ac.at) website has officially launched on 11th November 2024. Its aim is to consolidate the information about the [Machine Learning with Graphs](https://dm.cs.univie.ac.at/index.php?id=1911) work group to make our research more accessible to interested individuals.

For the more tech savvy people, here is a quick overview of the tech stack used on this website:

#### WordPress as our CMS

WordPress has undergone some substantial changes in the recent years. Most notably, it has evolved from a pure content oriented, static content management system, to a full fledged page-builder to the likes of Elementor or Divi.

Despite some concerning voices about WordPress’s shift towards the Full-Site Editing and the Gutenberg Editor, I believe that they are on the right track, albeit with several detours.

For this website it is the perfect solution for any group website. Let me explain:

##### Advantages:

1.  SEO Friendly
2.  User Friendly
3.  Easy to Manage
4.  Compatability
5.  Future Proof

##### Drawbacks:

1.  Flexibilty
2.  Security

#### Custom u:cris Plugin

To dynamically retrieve the current list of publications by our group, the University of Vienna has introduced a centralized management system called u:cris.
