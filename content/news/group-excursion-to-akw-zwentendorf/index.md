On Friday the 24th October 2025, the KriegeGroup set out to explore the Nuclear Reactor Facilities in Zwentendorf (Lower Austria).

\
The [Zwentendorf Nuclear Power Plant](https://www.zwentendorf.com/) was a fully built 700 MW boiling-water reactor near Vienna, completed in 1978 but never put into operation. On November 5, 1978, a referendum resulted in a narrow majority (50.47%) voting against its start-up, leading to the plant’s shutdown before it ever produced a single kilowatt-hour. Afterwards, Austria passed legislation banning nuclear power plants for electricity generation. Today, the site serves as a training center, event location, and technical curiosity, symbolizing Austria’s anti-nuclear stance.

![](/images/uploads/2025/10/2025-10-24-14.32.42-scaled-e1761661568866-1024x681.jpg)

Zwentendorf is the only nuclear power plant in the world that allows direct access to the nuclear core. Here you can see the moderator rods below the reactor.
