The paper **[Approximating the Graph Edit Distance with Compact Neighborhood Representations](https://link-springer-com.uaccess.univie.ac.at/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-70362-1_18)** by Franka Bause (DoCSunivie student, Cluster D&K), Christian Permann and Nils M. Kriege (DoCSunivie Supervisor, Cluster D&K) was accepted at ECML/PKDD 2024.

You can see the full version of the paper at [Springer Link](https://link-springer-com.uaccess.univie.ac.at/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-70362-1_18).

The **European Conference on Machine Learning and Principles and Practice of Knowledge Discovery in Databases** took place from September 9-13, 2024, in Vilnius, Lithuania.
