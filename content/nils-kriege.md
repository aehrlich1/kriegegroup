### Biography

#### Professional Experience

- since 2023: Associate Professor, Faculty of Computer Science, University of Vienna, Austria
- 2020–2023: Assistant Professor, Faculty of Computer Science, University of Vienna, Austria
- 2019/2020: Interim Professor (W3), Chair for Algorithm Engineering, Department of Computer Science, TU Dortmund, Germany
- 2015–2020: Postdoctoral Researcher, TU Dortmund, Germany
- 2015, 2016, 2018: Visiting Researcher, Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Group, University of York, UK (four months in total)
- 2010–2015: Research Associate, TU Dortmund, Germany

#### Academic Qualifications

- 2015: Doctorate in Computer Science, TU Dortmund
- 2009: Diploma in Computer Science, TU Dortmund

#### Individual Grants and Fellowships

- 2019: Vienna Research Groups for Young Investigators, project “Algorithmic Data Science for Computational Drug Discovery”, 1,466k € funding for an independent research group
- 2017: Member of the Global Young Faculty V, Stiftung Mercator and the Mercator Research Center Ruhr

#### Professional Services

- Regular PC memberships and review activities: NeurIPS, ICML, IJCAI, AAAI, ICLR, ICDM
- Journal reviews: Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery, Journal of Experimental Algorithmics
