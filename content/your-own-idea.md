# Your Own Idea!

Do you have a specific project in mind you want to investigate, that is not already listed on the thesis page? Then you can contact Prof. [Nils Kriege](mailto:nils.kriege@univie.ac.at) to propose your own project idea!

Your project idea must fulfill the following requirements:

- It must be related to graph learning techniques, e.g., graph kernels, graph neural networks, algorithms involving graphs, etc.
- It must be part of a Practical Course (P1, P2), a Bachelor thesis, or a Master thesis project
- It must be a reasonable amount of work for the type of project. A P1 project named “Solving the graph isomorphism problem in linear time” is not realistic. Similarly, a Master’s thesis that simply applies a kernel to some graphs is also not adequate. If you are unsure about the extent of the project required, we can happily help you out.

**Requirements:** Strong interest in graph learning.
